Problem

Selling a harvest is still a blind trade.

In India, 100M+ farmer families sell without seeing the market.

↳

the farmer keeps only ~33% of what the consumer pays

↳

₹1.5 lakh crore of produce is lost after harvest, every year

No shared price anchor, no payment protection — every deal starts from a guess, and the least-informed party loses.

RBI Bulletin 2022 · NABCONS / MoFPI 2022 CropBid · cropbid.in — 2

Solution

One exchange, everything a deal needs.

Live govt mandi anchor · 4,600+ mandis

Real wholesale prices (Agmarknet) on every listing — every state, refreshed daily. Everyone negotiates around the same number.

Open bidding + AI-agent negotiation

Buyers bid in transparent rounds — or each side's AI agent negotiates for them, round by round, within limits they set.

Escrow payments

Razorpay holds the money; it releases only on confirmed delivery. Sellers never ship and chase payments again.

Direct retail channel

Farmers flip a listing to retail at a fixed price; consumers buy any quantity instantly — farm to home, no bidding.

Live in production at cropbid.in — web storefront + native apps in Hindi, Marathi & English. CropBid · cropbid.in — 3
