# CropBid — 2-Minute Pitch

~290 words · ~2:00 at a natural speaking pace. Timing beats are guides, not stops.

---

**[0:00 — The problem]**

Namaste — I'm Hrishikesh, founder of CropBid.

In India, more than 100 million farmer families sell their harvest without ever seeing the market. The trader quotes a price, the farmer guesses — and ends up keeping only about a third of what the consumer finally pays. On top of that, one and a half lakh crore rupees of produce is lost every year after harvest. Every deal starts from a guess, and the least-informed party loses.

**[0:30 — The platform]**

CropBid fixes the two things every deal needs: a fair price, and safe payment.

CropBid is a crop exchange — live in production today at cropbid.in, on web and native apps in Hindi, Marathi and English. Every listing carries the live government mandi price from over 4,600 mandis, so farmer and buyer negotiate around the same public number. Buyers bid in open rounds — or each side's AI agent negotiates for them, within limits they set. And the money sits in escrow until delivery is confirmed — so a farmer never ships a truck and then chases the payment. Farmers can even flip a listing to direct retail and sell farm-to-home at a fixed price.

**[1:15 — The business]**

We're asset-light: we move the money, never the crop. No warehouses, no spoilage. We take a two percent fee on every escrow settlement — and because farmers, traders, wholesalers, retailers and consumers all trade on one exchange, every hop in the chain is a fee event.

**[1:40 — Where we are, and the close]**

Indian agritech is headed to twenty-eight billion dollars by 2030, and no one has built the farmer-first exchange. The platform is built; the next ninety days are about liquidity — one crop, one district, first GMV through escrow. Then we repeat the playbook.

We're incubated by India 2047 Ventures. See it live at cropbid.in. Thank you.
