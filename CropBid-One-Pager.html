The crop exchange for everyone.

Farmers, traders, retailers and consumers — trading around live government mandi prices, with money held safe in escrow until delivery.

Problem

Selling a harvest is still a blind trade.

100M+

farmer families in India sell without ever seeing the market.

~33%

is all the farmer keeps of what the consumer finally pays.

₹1.5 L Cr

of produce is lost after harvest, every single year.

No shared price anchor, no payment protection — every deal starts from a guess, and the least-informed party loses.

The Platform

One exchange, everything a deal needs.

Live govt mandi anchor · 4,600+ mandis

Real wholesale prices (Agmarknet) on every listing, refreshed daily — everyone negotiates around the same public number.

Open bidding + AI-agent negotiation

Buyers bid in transparent rounds — or each side's AI agent negotiates for them, round by round, within limits they set.

Escrow, and contact held until it funds

Razorpay holds the money and releases it only on confirmed delivery. The buyer's number reaches the farmer once the escrow is funded, so nobody takes the deal off-platform.

A farm-to-home shop, with a cart

Households fill one basket across several growers and pay one bill. Buyers can also post what they need and have farmers quote against it — and every seller and business buyer is approved by hand before they go live.

Live in production at cropbid.in — web storefront + native Android & iOS apps, with listings dictated by voice in Hindi, Marathi and English.

We move the money, never the crop.

A 2% settlement fee on every escrow release. Asset-light by design — no warehouses, no inventory, no spoilage. Every hop in the chain (farmer → trader → wholesaler → retailer) can clear on-platform, and each hop is a fee event.

A $28B market with an empty seat.

India agritech by 2030, growing ~25% CAGR. Incumbents solved one seat at the table — traders, logistics, or e-auctions inside the mandi yard. CropBid is farmer-first, asset-light, and puts the whole chain on one screen.
